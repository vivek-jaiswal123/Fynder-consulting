Withdrawal Types

Types of PF Withdrawal We Handle

Different situations qualify for different withdrawal types. We help you choose the right one.

Full Withdrawal (Retirement)

Withdraw your entire PF balance after retirement at age 58 or if you've been unemployed for 60+ days.

Form 19Form 10C100% Balance

Partial Withdrawal (Housing)

Withdraw up to 90% of PF balance for purchasing or constructing a house, or repaying a home loan.

Form 31Up to 90%Housing

Partial Withdrawal (Marriage)

Withdraw up to 50% of your share for self, son, or daughter's wedding after 7 years of service.

Form 31Up to 50%7+ Years

Partial Withdrawal (Education)

Withdraw up to 50% of your share for children's education after completing 7 years of membership.

Form 31Up to 50%Education

Medical Emergency

Withdraw up to 6 times your monthly wages or total employee share (whichever is lower) for major illness.

Form 31MedicalNo Lock-in

PF Advance (COVID-19 / Illness)

Special non-refundable advance for COVID-19, cancer, TB, or other specified illnesses — up to 3 months wages.

Form 31Non-refundableUAN-based
